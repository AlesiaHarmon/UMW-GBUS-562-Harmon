I checked your balance, and currently you have 4 PTO days remaining, which is 2 short of the 6 days you'd like for your honeymoon. Here are a couple of options:

1. **Borrow PTO you haven't earned yet**: You can borrow up to 3 PTO days from what you'll earn in the next six months. This would cover the extra 2 days you need. Borrowed days require your general manager's approval, and the request must be submitted at least 30 days in advance. Note that if you leave Ridgeline before earning those days back, their value would be deducted from your final paycheck. (Source: pto-policy.html, "Borrowing PTO you haven't earned yet")

2. **Submit your request early**: Since you're requesting 5 or more consecutive days off, your supervisor needs to approve it at least 30 days ahead of time (versus 14 days for shorter requests). Since your honeymoon is about two months out, you're in good shape timing-wise. (Source: pto-policy.html, "Requesting PTO")

I'd recommend submitting your PTO request (including any borrowed days, if needed) through your hotel's scheduling system as soon as possible to make sure it's approved well in advance.
