# Pop-Up Location Analysis for Wildframe Coffee Roasters

## 1–2. Weekly Purchases \& Four-Week Revenue

**Riverside Farmers Market**

* Weekly purchases: 9,000 × 2% = 180 buyers
* Weekly revenue: 180 × $14 = $2,520
* 4-week revenue: $2,520 × 4 = **$10,080**

**Union Station Concourse**

* Weekly purchases: 30,000 × 0.5% = 150 buyers
* Weekly revenue: 150 × $6 = $900
* 4-week revenue: $900 × 4 = **$3,600**

**Campus Library Atrium**

* Weekly purchases: 6,000 × 3% = 180 buyers
* Weekly revenue: 180 × $5 = $900
* 4-week revenue: $900 × 4 = **$3,600**

## 3\. Four-Week Rent \& Budget Check ($5,000 cap)

|Location|Weekly Rent|4-Week Rent|Within Budget?|
|-|-|-|-|
|Riverside|$900|$3,600|Yes|
|Union Station|$2,000|$8,000|**No — exceeds budget**|
|Campus Library|$600|$2,400|Yes|

## 4\. Revenue Minus Rent (Before Other Expenses)

|Location|Revenue|Rent|Revenue − Rent|
|-|-|-|-|
|Riverside|$10,080|$3,600|**$6,480**|
|Union Station|$3,600|$8,000|**−$4,400**|
|Campus Library|$3,600|$2,400|**$1,200**|

Note: Revenue minus rent is **not net profit** — it excludes staffing, inventory/COGS, equipment, insurance, and other operating costs that would apply at any location.

## 5\. Alignment with Core Business Model

* **Riverside**: Sells whole-bean bags — the same product line driving Wildframe's online and subscription sales. This audience is already buying in a format consistent with subscription conversion (e.g., "sign up and get this delivered monthly").
* **Union Station \& Campus Library**: Sell single cups — essentially a café transaction. This builds brand awareness but doesn't naturally funnel into bean sales or subscriptions, since cup buyers are seeking immediate consumption, not home brewing.

## 6\. Trade-offs

* **Union Station** has the highest foot traffic but the lowest conversion rate and highest rent, producing a net loss before other expenses and exceeding the rental budget outright. It is disqualified on cost grounds alone.
* **Campus Library** is budget-friendly and has the highest conversion rate, but low traffic and low average sale size cap total revenue, and the single-cup format doesn't support the bean/subscription funnel.
* **Riverside** combines moderate traffic, a strong conversion rate, and the highest average sale size, all within budget. It also produces by far the strongest revenue-minus-rent figure and sells the exact product (whole beans) that supports Wildframe's online and subscription growth — making it the most strategically aligned choice, not just the most profitable on paper.

Recommendation: Riverside Farmers Market
